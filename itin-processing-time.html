How Long Does an ITIN Take? Seven Weeks, Eleven Weeks or Longer from Abroad

You’ve sent your W-7 and it’s been six weeks. Is it lost, or is this normal?

Leila applied in February from London and is already nervous because her U.S. return is due in April.

The IRS timeframes

If the application is complete, the IRS aims to send an assignment letter in about seven weeks. During peak tax time (15 January–30 April), or when applying from abroad, it can take up to eleven weeks. Applications mailed from abroad may take considerably longer.

Getting your documents back

Originals are returned by the IRS (within about 60 days if you posted them). A prepaid express or courier envelope can speed return. If you used a CAA, your originals are back with you the same day.

What causes delay

  • Wrong reason box, or missing exception proof.
  • Name or date-of-birth mismatch between Form W-7 and your documents.
  • Expired documents, or missing visa pages.
  • Using the return’s own mailing address instead of ITIN Operations.

Checking status

If you haven’t heard after the referenced time, call the IRS (+1 267-941-1000 from outside the U.S., not toll-free). Keep a copy of everything you sent.

Practical takeaway

  • Plan for 7–11 weeks, longer from abroad.
  • Mistakes cause most delays, not the IRS queue.
  • Apply well before any deadline.

A reader asks

“Can you speed it up?”

We can’t change how fast the IRS works, but a correct, complete application, and no loss of time getting your originals back, is the best way to avoid avoidable delay.
