ITIN document rules

Do you need to send your passport for an ITIN application?

If you apply for an ITIN, the IRS requires identity and foreign-status documents. A passport is usually the strongest document because it can establish both identity and foreign status, but the route depends on how the document is verified.

The three compliant routes

  1. Meet a CAA in person. A Certifying Acceptance Agent can verify original documentation and return it after reviewing authenticity for eligible applicants.
  2. Send originals securely. If you cannot attend in person, original passports or IDs may need to be sent by secure tracked courier and returned by secure post.
  3. Use certified copies from the issuing agency. A certified copy must come from the original issuing agency and carry the official seal. Ordinary notarised copies are generally not accepted.

CAA verification is broadest for primary and secondary applicants. For dependents, IRS guidance limits what CAAs can verify, so we confirm the applicant type before advising whether the passport or another document can be certified through the CAA route.

Why video calls are not enough by themselves

A video call may help us identify you and review the case, but it does not replace the IRS document requirement. If we cannot physically inspect the original document or an issuing-agency certified copy, we will explain the compliant document route before submission.

For applicants outside the UK

If you are outside the United Kingdom, you can choose one of these pathways:

  • Travel to the UK for an in-person CAA verification appointment.
  • Arrange a meeting at Bedford, St Pancras International, Heathrow Airport or Luton Airport where suitable.
  • Send original documents by secure tracked courier for review and secure return.
  • Request certified copies from the issuing agency in your country, where available.

Official IRS references

The IRS Form W-7 instructions state that applicants must submit original documents or certified copies from the issuing agency, and explain that CAAs can verify original documentation and certified copies for eligible applicants. See the IRS Instructions for Form W-7 and the IRS ITIN documentation FAQs.
