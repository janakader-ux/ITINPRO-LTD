## Mandatory production rules

- Form numbers must be typeset as live SVG text, never generated inside AI photography.
- Use `itinpro.uk`, not a competitor name, in every footer and CTA destination.
- Keep all claims conditional: “may”, “review”, and “depends on circumstances”; never promise an ITIN or refund.
- Use “Certifying Acceptance Agent” only where the firm’s current IRS agreement supports it. Do not create an IRS seal, IRS logo, PTIN badge, or government-style endorsement badge.
- Passport wording must distinguish in-person verification from remote work. A video view alone is not sufficient where the CAA must physically possess the original or an issuing-agency-certified copy.
- Export each master as SVG, PNG, and WebP. Website masters are 1536 × 1024 (3:2); social crops are produced only after text-safe review.
