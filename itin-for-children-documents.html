ITIN for Children: Birth Certificates, Medical Records and School Records

Your baby has a passport, a vaccination card and a red book from the health visitor. Which one does the IRS accept?

Elena is applying for her 4-year-old, who is British. She wonders whether UK health records count.

Under-18s: birth certificate unless passport

Dependants under 18 must prove foreign status and identity with a civil birth certificate, unless a passport is submitted. Under 14 (under 18 if a student), no photo ID is required.

Medical records – under 6 only

Official shot/immunisation record, or a signed, dated letter from the provider on letterhead. It must show name, date of birth, address, provider’s name and address, and care dated within 12 months of the W-7. Records are used for foreign status only if the documents are foreign. If proof of U.S. residency is needed, the address must be in the U.S.

School records – under 24

A report card, transcript, or signed, dated letter from a school official on letterhead showing name, address, school details and term dates ending no more than 12 months before the application.

Original needed

If you submit school or medical records, the original must go with the application whichever route you use. Electronic copies sent by the school can be printed clearly for submission.

Practical takeaway

  • Under 18: birth certificate or passport.
  • Medical records only for under-6s; school records only for under-24s.
  • Records must be within 12 months and complete.

A reader asks

“Our red book has handwritten entries. Is that OK?”

The record must show all required details and be clear. We’ll review it against the IRS checklist before submission.
