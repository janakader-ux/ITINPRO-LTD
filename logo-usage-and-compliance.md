# ITIN Pro Final Refined Logo

## Recommendation
Use `itinpro-final-horizontal` for the website header, letterhead and email signature.
Use `itinpro-final-icon` for favicon, social profile and small spaces.
Use `itinpro-final-monochrome` for black and white printing.

## Why this version is stronger
- The icon is more distinctive and premium than a plain document/check mark.
- The gold arc suggests international movement and document routing without using US government symbolism.
- The typography is cleaner, wider and more confident for a professional tax brand.
- Use Certified Acceptance Agent support. Do not use IRS endorsed, IRS partner, official IRS agent, or official IRS seal/logo wording.
