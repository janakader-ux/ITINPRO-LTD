ITIN for 401(k), Pension and Annuity Payments Received in the UK

A U.S. pension provider is holding back 30% of your payment because it has no TIN for you. How do you stop that?

Janet worked in Chicago for ten years and now lives in Surrey. Her 401(k) administrator says it needs an ITIN to pay distributions.

Who can use Exception 1(d)

People receiving distributions such as pensions, annuities, rental income, royalties or dividends during the current year who must give an ITIN to the withholding agent (an investment company, insurance company, financial institution and so on) so it can meet tax withholding and reporting duties.

Entitled to payments after a family member’s death?

You become eligible to apply once you are entitled to a payment or distribution subject to information reporting and withholding. Your application must include an original document or signed letter from the withholding agent, on official letterhead, showing your name and confirming an ITIN is needed for distributions during the current tax year.

Completing Form W-7

  • Tick box h and enter “Exception 1d – Pension Distributions” or “Exception 1d – Annuity Distributions”.
  • If you claim treaty benefits (the UK has a treaty with the U.S.), also tick box a and give the treaty country and article number.
  • Submit with your passport and the withholding agent’s letter – no tax return required.

Practical takeaway

  • Exception 1(d) = no return, but an agent’s letter on letterhead.
  • Say “Pension” or “Annuity” on the box h line.
  • Treaty claim? Add box a plus country and article.

A reader asks

“My plan administrator will only send a statement, not a letter. Will the IRS accept it?”

Self-generated income statements are only accepted with a copy of the contract or a letter in a postmarked envelope from the withholding agent. We can send you a sample letter to request.
