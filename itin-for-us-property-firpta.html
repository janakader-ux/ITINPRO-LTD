US property and FIRPTA

Do you need an ITIN after selling US property?

A non-US seller may need an ITIN when US tax withholding has been applied under FIRPTA or where a US tax return is needed to report the sale and claim any refund due.

Typical documents

  • Executed sale contract or closing statement.
  • Form 8288 and 8288-A, where issued.
  • Passport or other acceptable identity/foreign-status documents.
  • 1040NR support where a US non-resident return is required.
