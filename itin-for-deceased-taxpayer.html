ITIN for a Deceased Taxpayer: What the Executor or Spouse Must Send

A relative abroad has died and left a U.S. tax refund unclaimed. Can you still get them an ITIN?

Fatima is the executor of her father’s UK estate; he had U.S. dividend income.

How to mark Form W-7

Write the word “Deceased” and the date of death across the top of Form W-7.

Three situations

  • Surviving spouse filing an original or amended joint return: Form W-7, return, and documents proving identity and foreign status of the deceased.
  • Court-appointed executor/administrator filing an original return: the same, plus a court certificate showing your appointment.
  • Someone else claiming a refund on a joint return where no executor was appointed: the same documents plus Form 1310 and a copy of the death certificate.

Children who died

If Form W-7 is for a deceased taxpayer under 18, one document must be a birth certificate unless a passport with a U.S. entry date is provided.

Practical takeaway

  • Write ‘Deceased’ and the date of death on Form W-7.
  • Documents depend on who you are.
  • Form 1310 and a death certificate apply where no executor is appointed.
