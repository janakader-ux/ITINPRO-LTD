Can You E-File With a New ITIN? The “Year Received” Rule

You’ve just got your ITIN letter. You open your tax software… and it won’t let you file. Why?

Ben got his ITIN in March and tried to e-file immediately.

The rule

You can only e-file a return using an ITIN in the calendar year after the year in which you received it. If you apply and receive an ITIN this year, you may not e-file any return (including prior-year returns) with that ITIN until next year.

Prior-year limits

Because of IRS e-file limits, you can only e-file the current tax year and two prior tax years.

So what do you do?

Your first return with the new ITIN is usually the paper return you attach to Form W-7. For later returns, check the year rule before choosing e-file.

Extensions and payments

Do not file Form W-7 with extension requests (Form 4868 or 2688) or estimated payments (1040-ES). Write “ITIN TO BE REQUESTED” where the ITIN or SSN is requested instead.

Practical takeaway

  • No e-file in the calendar year you receive the ITIN.
  • E-file works for the current and two prior years.
  • Never send W-7 with extension or estimate forms.

“I need to file an amended return. Can I e-file it?”
