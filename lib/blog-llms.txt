## Basics
- [What Is an ITIN Number and How Does It Work?](https://itinpro.uk/blog/what-is-an-itin-and-how-does-it-work): An ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) is a 9-digit number issued by the IRS, always starting with 9, for people who need a US tax ID but cannot get a Social Security Number. It is used only for US tax purposes. You apply on Form W-7, usually alongside a US tax return.

## Process
- [IRS Certifying Acceptance Agent Near Me in the UK](https://itinpro.uk/blog/irs-certifying-acceptance-agent-near-me-uk): An IRS Certifying Acceptance Agent (CAA) is authorised by the IRS to verify your identity documents for an ITIN application. The CAA checks your original passport, signs Form W-7 (COA) and sends the application, so your passport stays with you instead of going to the IRS.
- [ITIN Rejected? What CP566 and CP567 Mean and What to Do Next](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-rejected-cp566-cp567-what-next): An ITIN rejection is usually fixable. A CP566 notice means the IRS needs more information, so reply by the deadline on the letter. A CP567 notice means the application was rejected, so you normally send a fresh, corrected Form W-7 with the right documents and reason for applying.
- [How Long Does an ITIN Take from the UK in 2026?](https://itinpro.uk/blog/how-long-does-itin-take-from-uk-2026): An ITIN takes about 7 weeks once the IRS receives a complete Form W-7, or 9 to 11 weeks in peak season or from abroad, says the IRS. In our experience, timing follows the season: April to July can take 12 to 13 weeks, September about 7, and from October often 4 to 6.
- [How to Get an ITIN Without Sending Your Passport to the IRS](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-without-sending-passport-to-irs): You can get an ITIN without sending your passport to the IRS by applying through a Certifying Acceptance Agent. The CAA checks your original passport, certifies it on Form W-7 (COA), and sends that with your application. Your passport stays with you, and notarised copies aren't needed or accepted.

## Business
- [Do I Need an ITIN to Open a US LLC as a Non-Resident?](https://itinpro.uk/blog/do-i-need-an-itin-to-open-a-us-llc): An ITIN is not needed to form a US LLC or obtain its EIN. A foreign owner can apply for an EIN on Form SS-4 without one. An ITIN becomes necessary when the owner must file a personal US return, such as Form 1040-NR, or a bank, payer or partnership requires it.
- [ITIN for a Partnership Interest: A Guide for UK K-1 Investors](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-for-partnership-k1-uk-investor): An ITIN for a partnership interest lets a foreign partner be identified on Schedule K-1 and withholding forms, and file Form 1040-NR to claim credit for tax withheld. Under Exception 1(a), you can apply before filing a return, using evidence of the partnership interest.
- [ITIN vs EIN for UK Amazon Sellers: Which One Do You Need?](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-vs-ein-uk-amazon-seller): An EIN is a US tax number for a business, such as a US LLC, applied for on Form SS-4. An ITIN is a personal tax number for an individual who must file or be reported to the IRS but can't get an SSN. Many UK Amazon sellers need an EIN, an ITIN, or sometimes neither.

## Property
- [FIRPTA 15% Withheld? How Foreign Sellers Get It Back](https://itinpro.uk/blog/firpta-15-percent-withheld-get-it-back): A FIRPTA refund lets a foreign seller recover any excess of the 15% withheld from the gross sale price. You claim it by filing Form 1040-NR with Copy B of Form 8288-A. You need an ITIN to file, so apply early, ideally before closing.
- [Form 8288-B: Cut FIRPTA Withholding Before You Sell](https://itinpro.uk/blog/form-8288-b-reduce-firpta-withholding-itin): Form 8288-B is the IRS application for a withholding certificate, which can reduce FIRPTA withholding when you sell US property as a foreign person. It is filed before or at closing, and the seller needs an ITIN to apply. Starting the ITIN early gives you the best chance of keeping more cash at completion.
- [ITIN for a US Mortgage: What UK Buyers Need to Know](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-for-us-mortgage-uk-buyer): An ITIN for a US mortgage lets a UK buyer with no Social Security Number give lenders and the IRS a US tax ID. Where the mortgage interest is reported on Form 1098, you may apply under Exception 3 using your signed loan documents instead of a tax return.

## Tax returns
- [ITIN for 1040-NR: Do I Need to File a US Tax Return?](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-for-1040nr-do-i-need-to-file): An ITIN for 1040-NR filing is the number nonresidents use on their US tax return when they can't get a Social Security number. If you need to file or want a refund of US tax withheld, you usually send Form W-7 together with your first Form 1040-NR.
- [Received a 1099 as a Non-Resident? What to Do Now](https://itinpro.uk/blog/received-a-1099-as-non-resident-what-now): A 1099 sent to a non-resident usually means the payer has you on file as a US person or an undocumented account. Fix it by giving the payer a valid Form W-8BEN. If tax such as 24% backup withholding was taken, you normally reclaim it on Form 1040-NR, which needs an ITIN.

## Renewal
- [ITIN Renewal 2026: Has Your ITIN Expired?](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-renewal-expired-2026): An ITIN expires if it isn't used on a US federal tax return for three consecutive tax years. It expires on 31 December of the third year. You renew it with Form W-7, ticking 'Renew an existing ITIN', and normally attach the tax return you need to file.

## Family
- [ITIN for Spouse of a US Citizen: Filing Jointly from the UK](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-for-spouse-of-us-citizen-joint-return-uk): An ITIN for the spouse of a US citizen lets a non-US husband or wife be named on a joint US tax return. The spouse applies on Form W-7, ticking box e, usually sent with the first joint return, and their identity documents verified by a CAA or the IRS.
- [ITIN for Dependents: The 2026 Rules Explained](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-for-dependents-2026-rules): An ITIN for dependents is only worth getting if the child qualifies you for a US federal tax benefit. Since July 2025, the Child Tax Credit needs an SSN, but a child with an ITIN may still give you the $500 Credit for Other Dependents.

## Income types
- [ITIN for Form 8233: Treaty Relief for UK Consultants](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-form-8233-compensation-uk-consultant): An ITIN for Form 8233 lets a UK resident paid for personal services in the US claim a tax treaty exemption from withholding. Form 8233 needs a US taxpayer number, so without an SSN you apply on Form W-7 under Exception 2(a), with the Form 8233 and a letter from the payer.
- [ITIN for a Scholarship: A Guide for International Students](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-for-scholarship-international-student): An ITIN for a scholarship gives an international student a US tax ID when they receive scholarship or fellowship money but cannot get a Social Security Number. F-1 and J-1 students without work authorisation usually apply on Form W-7, often under Exception 2(b) or 2(c) with a letter from their school.
- [ITIN for Royalty Income: UK Authors and Musicians](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-for-royalty-income-uk-authors-musicians): An ITIN for royalty income is not always needed. Many UK residents can claim the US–UK treaty's 0% royalty rate on Form W-8BEN using a UK tax reference. An ITIN becomes necessary when you must file a Form 1040-NR to reclaim over-withheld tax, or when your payer insists on one.
- [US Tax on YouTube Earnings: Do UK Creators Need an ITIN?](https://itinpro.uk/blog/itin-for-youtube-adsense-creators-uk): US tax on YouTube earnings can apply to UK creators because some income may be treated as US-source. Most UK creators can submit Form W-8BEN tax info with a treaty claim and their UK tax reference, without an ITIN. You need an ITIN mainly to file a Form 1040-NR to reclaim over-withheld US tax.
- [US Executor Asking for Your ITIN? What UK Heirs Need to Know](https://itinpro.uk/blog/us-inheritance-executor-asking-for-itin): An ITIN for an inheritance is a US tax processing number that lets an executor, trustee or US custodian report and withhold correctly before paying a non-US heir. UK beneficiaries usually apply on Form W-7, often under Exception 1(d) with a signed letter from the withholding agent, rather than filing a return first.

You usually need an ITIN when you must file a US return, claim pension treaty benefits, or the broker insists in writing.

## Refunds
- [US Casino Winnings Taxed? How UK Residents Get a Refund](https://itinpro.uk/blog/us-casino-winnings-uk-refund-itin): A US casino tax refund for UK residents is usually possible because gambling winnings of UK residents are not taxed in the US under Article 22 of the US–UK treaty. To reclaim the 30% withheld, you file Form 1040-NR, which needs an ITIN applied for on Form W-7.
- [Expired W-8BEN and 24% Withheld on US Shares? Get It Back](https://itinpro.uk/blog/expired-w8ben-sold-us-shares-refund): An expired W-8BEN lets a US broker apply 24% backup withholding to share sale proceeds, or 30% to dividends. UK investors can usually reclaim it by filing Form 1040-NR, which needs an ITIN. Renew your W-8BEN straight away to stop further withholding.
