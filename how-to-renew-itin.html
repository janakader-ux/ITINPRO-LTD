How to Renew an ITIN: Form W-7, Documents and What Not to Forget

Your accountant says your ITIN has expired and your U.S. return will be held up. Do you start from scratch?

Nadia got her ITIN in 2012 and has not filed a U.S. return in years. Her U.S. income is now being reported again.

Renew only if you need it for a return

ITINs only need to be renewed if there is a federal tax filing purpose for the ITIN. If it will not appear on a U.S. return, you do not need to renew it now.

What to send

  • Form W-7, ticking “Renew an existing ITIN” and completing lines 6e and 6f (your previous ITIN and the name it was issued under).
  • A valid U.S. federal tax return, unless you qualify for an exception.
  • Your original identification documents or copies certified by the issuing agency (and the SSA denial letter if applicable).

Changed your name?

If your legal name has changed since the ITIN was assigned, send a letter explaining the change with proof: a marriage licence, divorce certificate or an official court document. The name on Form W-7 should match what you will use on your return.

Where it is processed

Renewal applications are processed at the IRS Austin office. Original documents are returned within about 60 days of receipt and processing. Using a certifying acceptance agent avoids that wait for most documents.

Practical takeaway

  • Renew only if the ITIN is needed for a return.
  • Include your old ITIN on line 6f.
  • A name change needs supporting documents.

A reader asks

“I’ve lost my ITIN letter. Can I still renew?”

Often yes. If you do not know your ITIN, tick “No/Don’t know” on line 6e. Tell us what you remember and we’ll guide you.
