What Is an ITIN? A Plain-English Guide for UK Residents

A U.S. bank, a buyer or an IRS form has just asked you for your “ITIN”. You live in the UK and have never heard the word. Where do you even start?

Priya inherited a share of a U.S. rental property. The management company emailed: “We need your ITIN before the next payment.” She has no Social Security Number and no idea what an ITIN is.

What an ITIN actually is

An Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) is a tax processing number issued by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to certain resident and non-resident aliens, their spouses and their dependants. It is a nine-digit number that starts with 9 and is formatted like a Social Security Number (9XX-7X-XXXX). The fourth and fifth digits fall in set ranges (50–65, 70–88, 90–92 and 94–99).

Why the IRS issues them

Both resident and non-resident foreigners can have U.S. tax filing and payment duties. The IRS issues ITINs so people who cannot get a Social Security Number (SSN) can still file returns, pay tax and have payments properly accounted for.

What an ITIN does not do

  • It does not authorise you to work in the U.S.
  • It does not give you Social Security benefits or the Earned Income Tax Credit.
  • It does not change your immigration status.
  • It is not valid identification outside the tax system – never offer it as ID for non-tax purposes.

Do I need one?

If you are eligible for an SSN you must not apply for an ITIN. If you cannot get an SSN but must give the IRS (or a U.S. payer) a taxpayer ID, you apply on Form W-7. The ITIN then goes in the space for the SSN on your U.S. return.

Practical takeaway

  • An ITIN is a 9-digit U.S. tax number for people who cannot get an SSN.
  • It is for tax purposes only: no work rights, no immigration effect.
  • Use Form W-7; apply with a U.S. tax return unless a listed exception applies.

A reader asks

“I live in Manchester and a U.S. company wants my ITIN. Can I apply from the UK without travelling to the States?”

Yes. You can apply by post, or through an IRS acceptance agent in the UK who can check your documents in person and send the package to the IRS.
