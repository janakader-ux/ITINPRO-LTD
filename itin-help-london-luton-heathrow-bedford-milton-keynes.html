ITIN Help in Bedford, Bedfordshire, Hertfordshire, Cambridgeshire, Buckinghamshire and London

Need your passport checked for an ITIN but can’t take time off work? Can an acceptance agent really come to your home or office – even at lunchtime?

Ana works in Bedford and can only spare her lunch hour. She wants her passport verified without taking a day off.

Where we meet

  • Home visits and office visits: we are flexible. If you are at work we can arrange the ID verification around your lunch break, or any time that suits you.
  • Bedford area: no travel charges, whether at your home, office or a public venue.
  • Milton Keynes: £20 travel charge.
  • Luton Airport meeting spot: £25 additional travel.
  • London location: £40 travel.
  • St Pancras station and central London: £45 additional travel.
  • Heathrow Airport meeting spot: £55 additional travel.
  • Europe: additional travel costs quoted by location.

Bedfordshire, Hertfordshire, Cambridgeshire and Buckinghamshire

We cover all four counties. Outside the Bedford area, a travel charge applies to cover the representative’s travel time and costs to visit you and collect your documents. For surrounding areas we confirm the travel cost by email before you pay.

Not near any of these?

We cover the whole UK. Choose “Elsewhere” on the form and one of our team will confirm availability and any travel charge.

Prefer to post your passport?

You can send your original passport to us and we return it by secure post. DHL charges a standard £60, and DHL/FedEx charges apply for other services.

Fixed fees

Our fees start from £395 all-inclusive with no VAT, for example £395 for a spouse application or renewal, £425 for an LLC or partnership, £450 for a U.S. property sale and £495 for a complex 401(k) case. These fees are for the ITIN application service only: preparing a U.S. tax return is a separate scope of work and an additional charge, quoted by our team based on complexity.

Practical takeaway

  • Six UK meeting points, plus Europe and ‘other UK’.
  • Posting your passport is possible, with secure return.
  • Fees start at £395, all-inclusive, no VAT.
