How to Apply for an ITIN from the UK: Step-by-Step

Can you really get a U.S. taxpayer number from your kitchen table in Leeds without flying anywhere?

Tom needs an ITIN to claim a refund of U.S. tax withheld on his pension. He is worried about posting his passport to Texas.

Step 1 – Decide your reason

Pick the single Form W-7 reason box that best explains your case (see our guide to why people need an ITIN). A tax purpose is required.

Step 2 – Return or exception

Attach a valid U.S. federal income tax return (Form 1040, 1040-NR or 1040-X) unless you qualify for an exception, in which case you attach proof of that exception instead.

Step 3 – Prove identity and foreign status

An original passport (or a copy certified by the issuing agency) is the only stand-alone document. Without a passport you need at least two other current documents, one with a recent photograph.

Step 4 – Choose how to submit

  • By post to IRS ITIN Operations, P.O. Box 149342, Austin, TX 78714-9342 (use the street address with Mail Stop 6090-AUSC for private couriers).
  • At an IRS Taxpayer Assistance Center in the U.S. by appointment – not practical from the UK.
  • Through an IRS-authorised Certifying Acceptance Agent (CAA), who can authenticate eligible original documents and return them after the in-person review.

Timing

If your application is complete expect an IRS letter in about seven weeks, up to eleven during peak tax time (15 January–30 April) or when applying from abroad. Applications mailed from abroad can take considerably longer.

Practical takeaway

  • One reason box, one return or exception, proper ID.
  • Never use the return’s own mailing address – send the whole package to ITIN Operations in Austin.
  • Using an eligible UK Certifying Acceptance Agent route can normally avoid sending an authenticated passport to the IRS.

A reader asks

“Do I have to include my passport itself or will a photocopy do?”

The IRS only accepts originals or copies certified by the issuing agency. A certifying acceptance agent can check your original and return it immediately.
