ITIN for a U.S. Mortgage: Exception 3 for First-Time Buyers

Your lender wants your ITIN to report your mortgage interest. You haven’t filed a U.S. return yet – can you still get one?

The Okafors are buying a holiday home in Orlando with a U.S. mortgage and are told: “Lender needs an ITIN before closing.”

Why the lender asks

Most home-mortgage lenders must report the interest they receive to both the IRS and the borrower on Form 1098 (Mortgage Interest Statement). First-time filers who secure a home loan can seek an ITIN to give to their lender.

What to provide

Documents showing evidence of a home mortgage loan on real property in the United States, such as a copy of the contract of sale or similar documentation. No tax return needs to be attached under this exception.

Form W-7

Tick box h and write “Exception 3 – Mortgage Interest”. Provide your passport and the mortgage evidence.

Practical takeaway

  • Exception 3 = no return, proof of a U.S. home mortgage.
  • Interest must be reported in the current tax year.
  • Apply as soon as the loan is secured.

A reader asks

“Can I wait to apply until after closing?”

Often the lender needs the number at or soon after closing. Contact us as soon as your loan is approved so timings line up.
