# ITIN PRO LTD — basic service enquiry forms

These are short website intakes, required for both **Contact me** and **Complete online**. They are internal enquiry records, not signed IRS forms or engagement letters. No payment is collected on these forms.

## Two short screens

1. Your contact details: first name, last name, email and phone (all required).
2. About your application: the questions for the selected service below, followed by privacy consent. Required questions have an asterisk. Optional questions are collapsed.

Both options save the same basic answers and send the staff notification. Contact me ends with **Please contact me**. Ready online application requests end with **Request my engagement letter**. Tax-return requests are passed to staff for a quote and discussion.

The U.S. visit questions follow the selected tax year and its two preceding years. They are optional approximate bands for enquiry triage; staff later requests exact dates/days when needed. Selecting 1040-NR does not determine residency or filing eligibility.

## Optional and private information

Location, referral source, approximate U.S. visits and additional notes are optional. Use Not sure where available instead of guessing. Citizenship dropdown records one country initially; other citizenships can be noted. Do not enter SSNs, ITINs, EINs, passport numbers or bank information in the public notes. Original signatures and CAA document verification remain separate from this intake.
