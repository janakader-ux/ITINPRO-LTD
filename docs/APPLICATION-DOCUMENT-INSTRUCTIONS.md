# ITIN PRO — application documents and signing instructions

Updated 12 October 2026. Checklist version 2026-10-12.2. These instructions are implemented in the sandbox portal; real-document collection is not yet enabled. The CAA selects the exact W-7 reason and exception after reviewing the facts. A service label or fee does not establish eligibility.

## Four separate client steps

1. Complete the internal preparation questionnaire and upload preparation scans when collection is enabled. The questionnaire is not filed as an IRS form.
2. Accept the case engagement letter electronically. This signature relates to the service agreement only.
3. Review the released draft and approve its accuracy, or request corrections. Approval applies to that exact version and does not sign W-7.
4. Print and wet-sign the final W-7, supply original supporting letters as instructed, and arrange CAA inspection of the original passport or acceptable issuing-agency-certified copy. A scan accepted for preparation does not complete identity verification.

## Passport scan for preparation

Provide a clear colour scan of the complete open passport spread, including the full photograph/data page and the facing top page, all four edges and the complete machine-readable lines. Avoid glare, cropping and fingers over information. Include relevant visa/entry pages when the route requires them. Each applicant needs their own identity evidence: a father's or other parent's scan does not verify a child's passport. The original verification step happens separately.

Passport-based preparation is the firm's preferred route. The IRS also permits specified alternative identity/foreign-status documents; any such case needs CAA review. Do not describe a passport as the only identity option accepted by the IRS.

## Supporting-letter signatures and blue ink

ITIN PRO requests an authorised signatory's original handwritten signature in BLUE INK on the actual issuer's official letterhead. Do not type, paste or digitally insert the signature. Upload a clear preparation scan and retain the original for dispatch/inspection instructions. Blue ink helps distinguish a handwritten original from a reproduced signature and reduces avoidable queries, based on the firm's case-handling experience. This is ITIN PRO policy, not a universal IRS ink-colour rule; no rejection percentage is claimed.

The CAA must approve any issuer-specific alternative. Do not impose this policy on every document: the IRS permits copies of certain contracts and specified electronically supplied medical/school records and statements. A pension account statement or an LLC letter alone does not automatically establish an exception.

The applicant normally signs W-7. A parent or court-appointed guardian may sign for a child under 18 who cannot sign, identifying the relationship. Court-appointed guardians supply court evidence. For an adult, a parent or spouse needs the applicable authority, including Form 2848 where required. Engagement e-signing and portal draft approval do not provide that authority.

## Important category rules

- **Spouse:** the standard spouse/joint-return service uses MFJ. MFS is not automatically rejected under every ITIN scenario: assess whether the applicant has their own federal filing obligation and choose the correct W-7 reason. Check any resident election with the tax preparer.
- **Dependent:** verify the actual allowable tax benefit, age and US residency evidence. Do not require a visa valid for over three months in every case. Medical records are limited to dependants under six; school records to students under 24, with the IRS content/recency conditions. A valid passport with an entry date can avoid an additional residency record, subject to the relevant rules.
- **Renewal:** attach the federal return for the year for which renewal is needed unless a documented exception applies. The last filed 1040/1040-NR is requested for internal review if available; it is not a substitute for the required return and is not a universal IRS renewal requirement. An expired ITIN used only on third-party information returns may not need renewal.
- **Partnership, effective 1 June 2026:** agreement pages must show partnership name, applicant name/signature and partnership membership/business in the US, with EIN on the agreement or accompanying CP575/147C. A supporting letter does not replace the agreement.
- **Pension/distribution:** the actual withholding agent must substantiate the current-year reporting/withholding need. A branded provider name alone does not establish eligibility.
- **Property seller:** distinguish buyer-submitted Forms 8288/8288-A from a withholding-certificate application on 8288-B. Do not require all three in every scenario.
- **Gambling:** Exception 2(d) requires an appropriate gaming official acting as an IRS ITIN Acceptance Agent. Without that official, assess the year-end 1040-NR/W-7/1042-S route.

- **Complete passport scan** (ITIN PRO): Upload a clear colour scan of the complete open passport spread: the photograph/data page AND the facing top page. Include all four edges, the full machine-readable lines and readable text. No half-page crops, glare or fingers over details. This scan is for preparation only. - **Recent proof of address** (ITIN PRO): Utility bill or other address evidence showing your name and full residential address. This is for our office records; it does not automatically satisfy dependent US-residency evidence.
- **US visa pages / I-20 / DS-2019, where relevant** (IRS; If held or required for the selected W-7 route): Upload the relevant visa and immigration documents if held or required for your route. Explain if you have no visa or used a visa-waiver route; the CAA will confirm applicability.
- **US entry / I-94 evidence** (IRS; If applicable to the selected route): Upload the relevant entry stamp or I-94 where available. State if you have never entered the US. Dependent residency requirements are reviewed separately.
- **Previous IRS rejection or correspondence** (ITIN PRO; If previously rejected or queried): Upload all relevant rejection notices or requests so that the reviewer can address the stated reasons.

### Dependent / child of a US citizen or resident

- **Relevant US federal tax return** (IRS): Upload the return for the tax year covered by this application, with relevant schedules. We will give separate instructions for the original signed return. Do not use the old years in a sample questionnaire.
- **Parent details, relationship and signing authority** (IRS): Provide the US person’s name and SSN/ITIN, the child’s relationship, birth details and parent/guardian signing authority.
- **Age-appropriate US residency evidence** (IRS): Where US residency must be proved and the passport lacks a US entry date: under age 6, provide a US medical record, US school record, US state ID or qualifying US visa; age 6 to under 18, a US school record, US state ID, US driver’s licence or qualifying US visa; age 18 or over, these permitted records (school records only under 24), or a US bank/rental/utility statement with the applicant’s name and US address. Residency exceptions apply for dependants of US military personnel stationed overseas and certain Canada/Mexico cases; Canada/Mexico applicants claiming the credit for other dependents still need US residency evidence. A visa is not universally mandatory and there is no blanket three-month visa rule.
- **Medical / school record details, when used** (IRS; If a medical or school record is used as identity/residency evidence): Medical records: under age 6 only; immunisation record or dated signed provider letter on official letterhead; child’s name, date of birth and address; doctor’s name and facility address; care within the preceding 12 months. School records: students under 24; report card, transcript or dated signed school letter with attendance/coursework and grades; student’s name/address, school name/address and term ending within the preceding 12 months. US addresses are required when proving US residency. A combination of official documents can satisfy these fields. Electronically received records printed for submission may be original; the CAA checks them.

Reviewer checks:

- Unlisted-category fee applies provisionally; confirm the case and fee before offering payment.
- Do not impose a blanket three-month visa rule. Check actual visa/entry/residency requirements and applicable exceptions.
- The child must qualify for the stated tax benefit. Parent/guardian authority and original W-7 signature require review.
