ITIN for an LLC Owner: What Counts as an LLC Interest?

You own part of a U.S. LLC from the UK and the accountant says “you’ll need an ITIN.” But for which of your three tax situations?

Olu is a one-third member of a Delaware LLC that owns a Florida rental. The LLC will issue him a Schedule K-1 and the bank needs a TIN.

Classification decides the route

How an LLC is taxed matters. Where an LLC is taxed as a partnership, a member who must be reported on IRS forms (such as Schedule K-1 or Form 8805) can use Exception 1(a) – third-party withholding on passive income – without attaching a tax return. If your LLC is taxed differently (for example a single-member disregarded entity), the ITIN normally travels with a filed return, and your U.S. tax adviser should confirm the filing position.

What to attach under Exception 1(a)

  • A copy of the portion of the partnership or LLC agreement showing the entity’s name, its EIN, and the applicant’s name and signature.
  • It must show the entity conducts business in the United States.
  • Reporting or withholding must take place within the current tax year.

How Form W-7 is completed

Tick box h and write “Exception 1(a) – A Partnership Interest” on the line. If you also claim a treaty benefit, tick box a and give the treaty country and article.

Practical takeaway

  • LLC taxed as partnership: Exception 1(a) with an LLC agreement extract.
  • Other LLC types: expect a return to be attached.
  • Reporting must happen this tax year.
