Does an ITIN Let You Work in the U.S.? No – and Here’s What to Do If You Can Work

A friend says “just get an ITIN and you can work.” Is that true? Spoiler: no.

Yusuf arrives on an F-1 visa and wants to take a campus job. A classmate told him to apply for an ITIN.

What an ITIN can’t do

An ITIN does not authorise work in the U.S. It is not valid for employment purposes, it does not change your immigration status and it does not entitle you to Social Security benefits or the Earned Income Tax Credit.

If you are allowed to work

Certain non-immigrant statuses authorise work, and some people must apply to USCIS for an Employment Authorization Document (EAD). Anyone with an EAD, or already authorised to work, is eligible for an SSN and should not apply for an ITIN unless the SSA rejects the SSN request, in which case the denial letter must be attached.

Students

Whether a student may work is set by USCIS rules; check with your Designated School Official or uscis.gov. Students who are eligible to work and will be employed should apply for an SSN; if denied, they may apply for an ITIN with the denial attached.

Practical takeaway

  • ITIN ≠ work permission.
  • Authorised to work? SSN first.
  • Use your DSO or USCIS for work-permission questions.

A reader asks

“I’m on a visa that lets me work part-time. Which number do I need?”

Almost certainly an SSN. We’ll help you check, and only proceed with an ITIN if the SSA has refused.
