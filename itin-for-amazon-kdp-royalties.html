Royalties and creators

ITIN support for Amazon KDP authors and US royalty income

If you receive US-source royalties from Amazon KDP, publishers, platforms or licensing arrangements, you may need to consider US withholding, treaty forms and whether an ITIN is required for your tax position.

Typical evidence

  • Royalty contract or publisher/platform statement.
  • US withholding documents, if issued.
  • Passport or acceptable identity/foreign-status documents.
  • W-7 exception evidence where a US tax return is not filed with the application.

How ITIN Pro helps

We review your royalty route, identify whether Form W-7 exception evidence is available, prepare the document checklist and arrange CAA verification where applicable.
