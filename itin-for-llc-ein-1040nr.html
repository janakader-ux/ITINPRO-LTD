LLC, EIN and 1040NR

ITIN and EIN support for non-US LLC owners

Non-US founders often confuse ITINs and EINs. An EIN identifies a business entity. An ITIN identifies an individual who needs a US taxpayer identification number but is not eligible for an SSN.

When an ITIN may be needed

  • You receive US partnership or LLC income reported on Schedule K-1.
  • You need to file Form 1040NR as a non-resident individual.
  • You need a US tax number connected to withholding, treaty or reporting requirements.
